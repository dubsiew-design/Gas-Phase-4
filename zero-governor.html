Gas safety devices & controls · No.14

Zero governor (air/gas ratio valve)

No air flow = no gas flow. The fan's suction draws gas through the governor in proportion to the air.

How it works
The fan draws air in through the venturi on the side of the fan housing. This creates a suction on the outlet of the 1:1 gas valve attached to the venturi. When the gas valve is energised, gas is drawn out of it, and the gas flow is proportional to the suction generated by the fan.
Balance of forces
The valve position is set by the adjustable setpoint (tension) spring, the counterspring, the pressure difference across the working diaphragm (referenced to atmosphere through the vent), the air impulse pressure, and the weight of the moving parts.
Offset adjustment
Set with the adjusting screw, best described as CO₂ against fan speed. A positive offset gives rising CO₂ as the fan slows and can still pass gas at zero air flow (potentially high CO). A negative offset gives falling CO₂ as the fan slows and stops gas before zero air flow (inherently safe).
Faults
Incorrect adjustment of the offset · incorrect adjustment of the throttle · failed system component. Always follow the manufacturer's instructions.
