What's built in
A multifunctional valve can include most of the controls an appliance needs in one compact unit: gas flow control, pressure regulation, flame protection, temperature control and burner ignition. Many also modulate the gas rate, which is common on combination boilers.
Operation and use
Main burner flame protection is usually by a thermocouple or flame rectification. On many natural gas models the working pressure can be set by the gas operative within the manufacturer's range.
Faults & checks
  • Check the flame protection device shuts down within the maximum time allowed.
  • Any gas control tap should operate smoothly.
  • “Buzzing” may indicate a faulty solenoid.
  • Test any disturbed gas joints with leak detection fluid.
  • Use the manufacturer's instructions for replacement part numbers.
