Gas safety devices & controls · No.12

Gas regulator (governor)

A compensated constant pressure regulator: a steady 21 mbar at the appliances, whatever the demand or inlet pressure.

The parts
Spring pushes on the main diaphragm, opening the valve. Main diaphragm has outlet pressure on its other side pushing back. Spindle links the diaphragm to the valve. Adjusting screw sets how hard the spring pushes, and so the outlet pressure. Compensating diaphragm: inlet pressure pushes on it opposite to the valve, so the two forces cancel and inlet changes don't move the valve.
The vent
The spring side of the diaphragm vents to outside air so it can move freely. If paint, dirt or insects block it, the regulator won't control the pressure correctly.
Pressures
Low pressure: up to 75 mbar. Medium: 75 mbar to 2 bar. Domestic working pressure: 21 mbar ± 2 (19–23 mbar). Standing pressure (no gas flowing): 30 mbar maximum.
Faults
Fails to lock out: call the emergency service provider and follow the Unsafe Situations Procedure.
Low or high working pressure: call the emergency service provider and follow the Unsafe Situations Procedure.
Membrane split and leaking gas: call the emergency service provider and disc ECV.
