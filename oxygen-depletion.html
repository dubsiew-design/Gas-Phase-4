Gas safety devices & controls · No.6

Oxygen depletion system (ODS)

How the precision pilot shuts the gas off before the room air becomes dangerous.

Also known as
ODD · Atmospheric sensing device (ASD) · Vitiation sensing device (VSD) · Oxypilot
Vitiation
The quality of the combustion air is impaired — not enough oxygen, too many products of combustion.
Where fitted
Mainly flueless space heaters and water heaters, where products of combustion could contaminate the room.
Service checks
  1. ODS is complete and not damaged.
  2. Unit is securely mounted in its recommended position.
  3. Aeration (air) port is clean and free from obstruction.
  4. Flame picture not distorted; sensor and cross-lighting flames correctly positioned.
Faulty pilot or thermocouple: exchange as a complete unit. Always follow the manufacturer's instructions.
