Gas safety devices & controls · No.13

Multifunctional valve — full sequence

Thermoelectric FSD, regulator and solenoid valve in one body, from lighting the pilot to full shut-down. The parts doing the work in each step light up in yellow.

One valve, several controls
This valve combines controls covered separately: a thermoelectric FSD (push button, interrupter, magnet and thermocouple), a regulator (diaphragm, spring and valve) and an electrically operated solenoid valve switched by the thermostat or boiler control.
Lock-up
With no gas flowing, outlet pressure builds on the diaphragm and pushes the regulator valve shut (lock-up). When the solenoid opens and gas flows, the pressure drops and the spring opens the valve to regulate.
Testing the FSD
  • Connect a pressure gauge to the outlet test point.
  • Light the burner at full rate, then turn off the gas at the inlet.
  • Time for 60 seconds, or until you hear the valve operate (audible click), then turn the gas back on.
  • There should be no momentary movement (“blip”) on the gauge when the gas is turned back on.
